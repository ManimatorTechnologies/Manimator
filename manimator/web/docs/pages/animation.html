Steps

The Animation panel under the canvas shows the scene's steps as a timeline. They play in order: each step plays one or more animations at the same time, or waits.

Adding animations

Select an object and press + in the Animation panel. Pick an animation from Appear, Disappear, Emphasize, Transform or Move, or animate one of the object's methods. It's added after the selected step.

Scene actions

With no object selected, + offers the scene's own actions, such as moving the camera or transforming the grid. Which ones depends on the scene type.

Arranging steps

The buttons beside +:

  • add a pause,
  • play the selected step together with the one before it,
  • split a step back into separate steps,
  • move it earlier or later,
  • and delete it.

Drag the right edge of a step to change how long it lasts (Shift: half seconds).

Editing a step

Click a selected step again to edit it:

  • its animations, and the object each one animates,
  • Easing: how the motion speeds up and slows down,
  • Stagger: 0 plays everything together; higher values start each animation a little after the one before.
