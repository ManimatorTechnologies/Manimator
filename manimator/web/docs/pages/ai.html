Connectors

Manimator has two connectors (MCP servers). The Account page shows them too.

ConnectorWhat it does
GuestQuick animations inside the chat. No account needed.
Your accountYour AI makes and changes projects in your account.

Connecting

In your AI app, add a custom connector and paste one of the addresses above. The account connector asks you to sign in to Manimator and to allow it when you connect.
