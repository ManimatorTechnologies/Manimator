Presentations need an account.

Making a presentation

On the Presentations page, make a new one from a project. Then line up its scenes in any order, with videos you upload in between.

Presenting

Press Present to play it full screen. Each item plays once and holds on its last frame. A click pauses or plays.

How scenes play

On the Account page, choose how a presentation's scenes are played:

  • Live: drawn as they play.
  • Render first: every scene is made before you start.
  • Stored: plays renders kept in your account, first making and saving any that are missing or out of date.
