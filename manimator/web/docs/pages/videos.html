Playing in the editor

Press Space or F5 to play the scene on the canvas. Press it again, or Esc, to stop.

Making videos

Render scene (F6) makes the current scene's video. Render all (F7) makes one video for each scene. Then play or download them from the window that shows them.

Videos are made in your browser, so keep the tab open until they finish. Downloads are MP4 files, or WebM where a browser can't make MP4.

Project file

Download (Ctrl+S) saves a copy of the project as a .manimator file.

Python for Manim

Python (Ctrl+E) downloads a zip with scene.py, the pictures and fonts it uses, and a README. It renders with Manim on your own computer, and you can keep editing the code by hand:

manim -pqh scene.py MyScene

Replace MyScene with the name of a scene.
