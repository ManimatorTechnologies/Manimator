With the mouse

  • Drag a number's label sideways to change it.
  • Hold Shift while placing or dragging an object to switch snapping on or off.
  • Double-click an object's name in the Objects list to rename it.
  • Right-click a scene tab to duplicate, move or delete the scene.
  • Drag the right edge of a step to change how long it lasts.
