# Refraction

A ray of light passing from one medium into another and bending by Snell's law,
sin(θ₁) / v₁ = sin(θ₂) / v₂. The ray hits the boundary at the object's center, and stops at the edges of the
media, so moving the object moves the whole picture.

- **Incoming angle**: the angle θ₁ between the ray and the normal line. Below 0 the ray comes in from the right.
- **Top speed** and **Bottom speed**: how fast light travels in each medium. The bent angle θ₂ follows from them.
  Light going into a slower medium bends towards the normal, into a faster one away from it.
- If the bottom is faster and the angle is past the critical angle, the light can't get through: the ray is
  reflected back up and the bottom arc and label are left out.
- **Normal line**, **Angle arcs** and **Angle labels** can each be turned off. The labels are LaTeX.
- Width, depth, ray length, colors and line widths can all be changed. The top medium is see-through until you
  raise its opacity.

Its parts, in order: top medium, bottom medium, normal line, incoming ray, outgoing ray, top arc, bottom arc,
top label, bottom label. A part that is turned off is an empty group, so the order never changes.
